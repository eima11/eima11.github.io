# Flex Academy landing page design brief

Design assignment for Product / Brand Designer candidates: a landing page for Flex Academy, timeboxed to 6 to 8 hours over 7 days.

## Context
- **The Flex** (theflex.global): fully furnished, move-in-ready apartments for short and long stays in London, Paris, Lisbon, Berlin, Algiers, Oran and Constantine, with Milan and Cairo coming. Serves travellers, relocating professionals and 150+ corporate housing partners. Tagline: "Flexible Living, Made Easy."
- **Base360** (base360.ai): property management software the founders built to run The Flex, sold to other STR operators: channel manager (130+ platforms), AI pricing, unified inbox, cleaning and maintenance, CRM, finance, HR. Tagline: "Run your Short Term Rentals on AI autopilot." Primary CTA: Book a demo.
- Founders: Raouf Yousfi and Michael Buggy.
- **Flex Academy (new)** helps people start and grow their own short-term rental company using the founders' playbook, systems and software. All English-speaking countries. Two offers:
  - **Learn how to launch** (entry price X): newcomers starting their first STR business. Bigger market. Sold through a **free webinar**.
  - **Learn how to scale** (premium price Y): operators who already run units and want to grow into a real company. Sold through a **strategy call**.

## Audience
1. **The 9-to-5 leaver** (Launch): employed, wants an income they own; no units or one; needs a safe playbook. Bigger market.
2. **The early operator** (Scale): runs a few units, stuck on manual work, thin margins, no time to grow.
Ambitious, busy, sceptical of "guru" courses; comparing against YouTube, cheap courses and expensive masterminds.

## The job of the page
- Launch conversion: "Register for the free webinar": name, email, country → confirmation with date and add-to-calendar.
- Scale conversion: "Book a free strategy call": 20–30 min, qualified by units today, target, city, budget band → calendar step → confirmation.
- Secondary: waitlist or free "STR scaling checklist" by email.
- Success: understood in 5 seconds, trusted within 30 seconds, can act at any point of the scroll.

## Scope
- Required: desktop 1440 px full scroll; mobile 390 px (hero + one section); both CTA flows (button state, form/calendar, confirmation); a 1-page design notes frame (audience, structure, 3 key decisions) or Loom.
- Optional: hero motion concept; dark mode or alternative art direction for one section.
- Out of scope: logo redesign, full brand system, all copy, development.

## Offer details (placeholder)
- Launch at X: self-paced course + live group sessions + templates to open first units.
- Scale at Y: 12-week programme, weekly group sessions, monthly 1:1 with a founder, operator community, SOPs, Base360 access.

## Deliverables
1. Figma link (one file, clearly named frames, design notes frame).
2. Loom (max 5 min): who it's for, structure and why, 3 proudest decisions, one thing to test first after launch.
3. Optional: what you'd do with 2 more days.
